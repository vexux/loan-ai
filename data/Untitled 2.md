# ICICI Bank Retail Loan Policy Guidelines

## 1. Personal Loan Eligibility Criteria

### Salaried Applicants
- **Age:** Minimum 20 years, Maximum 58 years
- **Minimum Net Monthly Income:** ₹35,000
- **Minimum CIBIL Score:** 700
- **Employment Requirement:** Minimum 1 year total work experience
- **KYC Requirement:** KYC verification must be complete (verified = 1)
- **Account Status:** Active account in good standing

### Self-Employed Applicants
- **Age:** Minimum 23 years, Maximum 65 years (at maturity)
- **Minimum Annual Turnover:** ₹15,00,000
- **Minimum CIBIL Score:** 720
- **Business Vintage:** Minimum 2 years in current business

### Product Limits
- **Maximum Loan Amount:** ₹50,00,000
- **Maximum Tenure:** 72 Months
- **Collateral Required:** None

---

## 2. Home Loan Eligibility Criteria

### Salaried Applicants
- **Age:** Minimum 23 years, Maximum 60 years
- **Minimum Net Monthly Income:** ₹50,000
- **Minimum CIBIL Score:** 700
- **KYC Requirement:** KYC verification must be complete (verified = 1)
- **Account Status:** Active account

### Self-Employed Applicants
- **Age:** Minimum 28 years, Maximum 70 years
- **Minimum Annual Gross Income:** ₹8,00,000
- **Minimum CIBIL Score:** 720
- **Business Vintage:** Minimum 3 years continuous business

### NRI Applicants
- **Age:** Minimum 21 years, Maximum 65 years
- **Overseas Employment:** Minimum 1 year continuous overseas employment

### Product Limits
- **Maximum Loan Amount:** ₹5,00,00,000
- **Maximum Tenure:** 30 Years

---

## 3. Car / Vehicle Loan Eligibility Criteria

### Salaried Applicants
- **Age:** Minimum 20 years, Maximum 65 years
- **Minimum Net Monthly Income:** ₹30,000
- **Minimum CIBIL Score:** 650
- **KYC Requirement:** Mandatory

### Self-Employed Applicants
- **Age:** Minimum 21 years, Maximum 70 years
- **Minimum CIBIL Score:** 750
- **Income Proof:** ITR for previous 2 years required

### Product Limits
- **Maximum Loan Financing:** Up to 100% on-road price
- **Maximum Tenure:** 84 Months

---

## 4. Universal Blanket Rules (All Loans)

- **Absolute Disqualification:** Any applicant with a CIBIL / Credit Score below 500 is automatically rejected for all loan products.
- **KYC Incomplete:** Applications with unverified KYC cannot be approved.
- **Account Status:** Inactive accounts are ineligible until reactivated.
